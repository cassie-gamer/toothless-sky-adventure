# Toothless's Sky Adventure 🐉☁️

Fly through the sky and rescue 5 lost dragon eggs!

## The Story

Toothless (or the Light Fury!) discovers **5 lost dragon eggs** scattered across **5 floating sky islands**! Fly between the islands, steer around storm clouds, dive through glowing wind rings for speed boosts, and carry each egg home to the nest!

When all 5 eggs are home... **they ALL hatch!** 🐣

## How to Play

- **Steer** with WASD / arrows (or drag on touch screens)
- **Dive DOWN** to fly faster!
- Fly through **glowing wind rings** for speed boosts
- Avoid **storm clouds** - they slow you down!
- Land on each island to grab its **lost egg**, then carry it home to the nest
- Rescue all **5 eggs** to win!

## Unity Setup

1. `SkyFlight.cs` - on your dragon (Toothless or Light Fury)
2. `SkyIsland.cs` x5 - floating islands, each with a `DragonEgg.cs`
3. `StormCloud.cs` - place storm clouds between islands
4. `WindRing.cs` - place glowing rings along flight paths
5. `SkyAdventureManager.cs` - on a game manager, hook up the nest + UI text

Have fun flying! 🐉
